Corrections

  1. शठे मित्रं ❌ → शठं मित्रं ✔️

  2. सप्त च गृहे ❌ → ससर्पे च गृहे ✔️

    • "ससर्पे" means "in a house with a snake."

  3. Traditional punctuation:

    • Use ॥५॥ rather than ।।५।।

So the corrected verse is:

दुष्टा भार्या शठं मित्रं भृत्यश्चोत्तरदायकः ।
ससर्पे च गृहे वासो मृत्युरेव न संशयः ॥५॥
— Chanakya

Meaning: A wicked wife, a deceitful friend, an insolent servant, and living in a house with a snake—these are surely causes of death; there is no doubt about it. openpathshala.com+1

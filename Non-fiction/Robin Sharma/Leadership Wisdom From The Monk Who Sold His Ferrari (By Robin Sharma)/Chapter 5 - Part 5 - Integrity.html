# Integrity is doing what you say and saying what you do.

# Leadership builds on the principle of integrity, not "ends-justify-the-means" philosophy.

# Visionary leaders care less about appearing right than doing right.

# The leader with integrity will never let his lips betray his heart and will always let his principles guide his actions.

# So what are these principles that someone can a great leader stands for?
There are the Gandhian factors: Honesty, Industry, Patience, Perseverance, Loyalty, Courage and Humility.

# Principles are to people what roots are to trees. Without roots, trees fall when they are thrashed by the winds. Without principles, people fall when they are shaken by the gales of existence."
— 19th Century Spanish Philosopher Carlos Reyles
